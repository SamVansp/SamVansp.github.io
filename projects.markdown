    <div class="slide" data-title="Buffalo River Maps"
         data-desc="One of my first projects at the start of my term was to support my supervisor in the creation of a new Management Plan for Buffalo River State Park. This is a big deal, as park management plans are only rewritten in the event of a major change at the park, and the plan for this park dates back to the park's creation in 1979. As part of this process, a Citizen Advisory Committee was convened, and I assisted with running these meetings throughout the spring and summer. I also supported the process through writing portions of the plan as well as editing the text. My most significant contribution was the creation of the maps that would go along with this plan. Overall, I created dozens of iterations of the 12 maps that would support the management plan. These included such diverse themes as topography, recreational opportunities in the surrounding area, and proposed developments in the plan."

    <div class="slide" data-title="State Park Vehicle Counters"
         data-desc="The Parks and Trails system at the DNR currently relies on a mix of technologies to calculate visitation. I participated in the current effort to modernize the system through the installation of cutting-edge vehicle counting technology in a pilot program last spring. That led to a full implementation of the system this summer. As my advisor and the original project leader was on maternity leave, and a new planner would be coordinating the project for the spring, the task of preparing for and transitioning knowledge about the installation project fell to me. This involved communicating with over 60 park managers about the siting of their counters, ensuring that the appropriate permits were in place, coordinating the training of the installation team, and passing on the insights that had been gained during the pilot program. Once the summer installation season began, I also co-led the installation process, spending every other week on the road installing the counters at every state park across Minnesota."

    <div class="slide" data-title="Accessibility Data Collection"
         data-desc="Helping visitors determine the accessibility of Parks and Trails facilities before visiting is an ongoing challenge facing the DNR. To try to combat this, I led an effort to collect and publicize data on the accessibility, or lack of accessibility, of facilities across the state so that visitors can make informed decisions about where to recreate. To do this, I created an innovative survey tool using Survey123 Connect and ArcGIS QuickCapture that will allow for the streamlined collection and processing of data on 18 types of facilities, from campsites to fishing piers. This project involved writing the code for the survey, testing the survey, and coordinating the accessibility working group, whose thoughts informed the direction of the project. It also included creating guides and how-tos that will allow for the rollout of the tool to the entire parks and trails system. Overall, this experience has given me valuable insights on project management and the challenges of standardizing data collection on such a wide array of facilities."
