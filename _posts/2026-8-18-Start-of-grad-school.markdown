This August, I wrapped up my second term of service with the Conservation Corps of Minnesota and Iowa. In my role with the Corps, I worked alongside the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources Part and Trails Planning and Development units. Over the past year and a half, I've gained incredibly valuable experience and led several important projects to completion. One major milestone was developing a GIS application to collect accessibility information on park facilities. I also helped launch initiatives with significant implications for the future, including the installation of vehicle counters at over 60 state parks over the course of the summer.

I am leaving this position for an exciting new opportunity: pursuing my Master’s degree at the University of Michigan School for Environment and Sustainability. I will be in the Geospatial Data Sciences track, and I hope to build upon my experiences with the DNR and build futher skills throughout Graduate School.
