# About Me

<div class="about-section hero">
  <img src="/assets/images/fieldwork2.jpg" alt="Fieldwork photo" class="hero-img">
  <div class="hero-text">
    <p>I am currently attending the University of Michigan Ann Arbor, where I am getting my Master's in Geospatial Data Sciences from the School for Environment and Sustainability. I graduated from Macalester College in 2024, where I earned my B.A. in Environmental Studies and Data Science. While in school, I thoroughly enjoyed blending my passion for data visualization and analysis with environmental conservation and stewardship. This was exemplified by my capstone project, where I worked with <a href="https://fmr.org/updates/conservation/spring-intern-creates-strategic-land-protection-database" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Friends of the Mississippi River</a> to identify and map high-value conservation areas across the Twin Cities region.</p>

    <h2>Current Work</h2>
    <p>I am currently attending graduate school and am excited about all the new opportunities that will bring. I recently finished 1.5 years of work with the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources as an individual placement with the Conservation Corps of Minnesota and Iowa. In this role as a Planning and Development Specialist in Parks and Trails, I was involved in a variety of projects. Some of my favorites include developing a survey tool to collect accessibility data at state parks, installing and validating vehicle counters across the state park and trail system, and supporting long-term planning efforts such as the <a href="https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/input/mgmtplans/parks/buffalo-river/buffalo-river-plan-2026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Buffalo River State Park Management Plan</a> and the <a href="https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/aboutdnr/reports/scorp/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">State Comprehensive Outdoor Recreation Plan</a>.</p>
